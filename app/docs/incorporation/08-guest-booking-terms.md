# Guest Booking Terms — Holons Coop

Effective [date]. These terms apply to every guest who books a stay, a retreat or an event at the
Holons Coop park.

> DRAFT — counsel review required before any booking is accepted. The park may not yet be open or
> permitted; no booking is an assurance that a stay can be honoured.

## 1. Booking and eligibility

1.1 Bookings are made through the Co-op's booking channel. The lead guest must be at least 18 and
provide a valid identity document and vehicle details.

1.2 The lead guest is responsible for the booking, payment and the conduct of everyone in the party.

1.3 The Co-op may refuse or cancel a booking for inaccurate information, capacity limits, safety,
maintenance or agency requirements.

## 2. Rates, fees and tax

2.1 The nightly rate is the rate shown at booking. Rates may change for new bookings but not for a
confirmed booking.

2.2 The published 8% transient occupancy tax applies to taxable stays and is shown separately. It is
remitted by the Co-op.

2.3 Additional charges may apply for extra vehicles, pets where allowed, late departure, damage or
excessive cleaning, as stated at booking.

## 3. Payment and deposit

3.1 Full payment is due at booking unless the listing states otherwise. A booking is confirmed only
when payment clears.

3.2 A refundable damage deposit of $[____] may be required and is returned within [__] days after
departure, less documented damage.

## 4. Cancellation and changes

4.1 A guest may cancel in writing. Cancellations at least 14 days before arrival receive a refund of
amounts paid, less a $[____] administrative fee. Cancellations within 14 days are non-refundable
unless the Co-op can rebook the nights.

4.2 Changes are subject to availability and any rate difference.

4.3 If the Co-op cancels for maintenance, safety, weather, fire, water supply or agency action, the
guest may choose a full refund of amounts paid or a credit for a future stay.

## 5. Arrival and departure

5.1 Check-in is from [time] and check-out is by [time]. Early arrival and late departure require
approval.

5.2 Guests must register at arrival, display any vehicle pass, and follow directions to the assigned
space and parking.

5.3 The guest must leave the space clean, remove trash and personal property, and report damage
before departure.

## 6. Occupancy and vehicles

6.1 Occupancy is limited to [__] persons per space, including children. Extra guests require
approval and may incur a fee.

6.2 One recreational vehicle and one towed or towing vehicle may use the assigned space. Extra
vehicles must use visitor parking near the entrance.

6.3 Parking on roads, fire lanes, accessible spaces without a permit, or unassigned ground is
prohibited. Parking is never internal to the residential areas.

6.4 Tents, temporary structures and exterior storage are not permitted without written approval.

## 7. Park rules

Guests must:

- Observe quiet hours from [time] to [time].
- Follow fire and heat restrictions; open fires and fireworks are prohibited.
- Use only designated waste and greywater disposal points.
- Keep pets on a leash where pets are permitted, and clean up after them.
- Avoid generators outside posted hours.
- Respect shared facilities, event limits and private spaces.
- Refrain from weapons, illegal substances and conduct that endangers others.

## 8. Events and retreats

8.1 Event bookings are subject to attendance caps, noise limits, insurance requirements and agency
conditions.

8.2 The organiser is responsible for the conduct of attendees and for any damage or additional
cleaning.

8.3 Alcohol, amplified sound and food service require written approval and compliance with the
Co-op's food-safety classification.

## 9. Liability and insurance

9.1 Guests use the park at their own risk. To the extent permitted by law, the Co-op is not liable
for loss or injury not caused by its negligence or wilful misconduct.

9.2 The Co-op is not responsible for guests' vehicles, RVs or personal property. Guests should
insure their own property.

9.3 The guest indemnifies the Co-op for claims arising from the guest's conduct or property.

## 10. Damage and removal

10.1 The guest is responsible for damage beyond ordinary wear and for excessive cleaning.

10.2 The Co-op may remove a guest, without refund, for a serious breach, endangerment, overcrowding,
unauthorised vehicles or nonpayment. Removal does not waive amounts owed.

## 11. Privacy and data

The Co-op collects contact, vehicle and payment information to manage the booking, comply with tax
and safety requirements, and communicate about the stay. It does not sell guest data. Guests may
request access or correction under the Co-op's privacy practice.

## 12. Force majeure

The Co-op is not liable for failure to provide a stay caused by fire, flood, heat, storm, road
closure, water or power failure, epidemic, government action or other events beyond its reasonable
control. In those cases the cancellation provisions in section 4.3 apply.

## 13. Governing law and disputes

California law governs. The parties will attempt to resolve a dispute in good faith before pursuing
remedies. The Co-op may seek injunctive relief to protect the property or safety.

## 14. Acceptance

By booking, the guest confirms that the guest has read these terms, the park rules and the
cancellation policy, and accepts them for everyone in the party.
