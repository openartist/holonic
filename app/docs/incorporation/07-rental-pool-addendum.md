## 1. Purpose

The Co-op manages a rental pool for members who want to release unused space-nights to guests. The
Co-op handles booking, collection, guest rules, cleaning and settlement. The Member receives the
Member's share of lodging receipts for nights actually booked and paid.

## 2. Opt-in and revocation

2.1 The Member opts in by signing this addendum and setting availability in the Co-op's calendar.

2.2 The Member may revoke participation on 30 days' written notice. Revocation does not affect
bookings already confirmed, which will be honoured and settled.

2.3 The Co-op may suspend the pool or a Member's participation for maintenance, insurance, agency
action, nonpayment, rule breaches or safety.

2.4 A resident Member may release nights while away. Releasing nights does not end the Member's
tenancy, change the Member's occupancy type or treat the space as vacated, and the Member keeps the
right to return to it.

## 3. Availability and release

3.1 The Member sets available dates in the calendar before the published release deadline. Dates not
released remain for the Member's own use.

3.2 The Co-op may release only the dates the Member has released. The Co-op may set minimum stays,
season windows and blackout dates.

3.3 The Member keeps priority for the Member's own stays. Once a date is released and booked, the
Member may not reclaim it except by agreement and any cancellation cost.

## 4. Rental split

4.1 The Member receives **60%** of lodging receipts for nights booked through the pool.

4.2 The Co-op retains **40%** of lodging receipts, out of which it pays the direct costs of the
booking.

4.3 Direct costs are charged as stated in Schedule 1. The board may change the schedule on 30 days'
notice before the next season, and must disclose the change before the Member releases dates.

4.4 The split applies to lodging receipts only. Venue fees, event fees and parking receipts belong
to the Co-op, which bears their direct costs.

## 5. Transient occupancy tax

5.1 The Co-op collects the published 8% transient occupancy tax on taxable stays, holds it as a
liability, and remits it to the county on the required schedule.

5.2 Tax is not revenue and is not included in the lodging receipts used for the split.

5.3 The Co-op will issue statements and tax forms for owner payouts as the CPA advises. The Member
is responsible for the Member's own income tax.

## 6. Settlement

6.1 The Co-op settles owner shares monthly during the season, within [__] days after the end of each
month, by the method the Member selects.

6.2 Each statement shows nights booked, gross lodging, the split, direct costs, tax collected and
the net payout.

6.3 The Co-op may withhold a payout to cover damage, chargebacks, unpaid guest balances or a
disputed charge, and will explain the withholding in writing.

6.4 A payout below $[__] may be carried forward to the next settlement.

## 7. Guest conduct and damage

7.1 Guests must accept the guest booking terms and follow the rules. The Co-op may remove a guest who
breaches them.

7.2 The Member is responsible for damage to the space or shared property caused by the Member's
guests, beyond ordinary wear, to the extent not recovered from the guest or insurance.

7.3 The Member must leave the space in the condition required by the rules before a booked stay.

## 8. Insurance and liability

8.1 The Member must maintain RV and vehicle insurance and cooperate with any claim arising from a
stay in the Member's space.

8.2 The Co-op maintains park and general liability insurance but is not an insurer of the Member's
property or income.

## 9. Records and privacy

9.1 The Co-op keeps booking, payment and tax records for at least seven years, and gives the Member
access to the Member's own statements.

9.2 The Co-op may share guest contact details with the Member only as needed to prepare for a stay,
and must handle guest data under its privacy practice.

## 10. No guarantee

The Member acknowledges that occupancy, nightly rates, season length and payouts vary; that the
study's figures are illustrative; and that the Co-op does not guarantee any booking, occupancy
level, revenue or payout.

## 11. Term and termination

This addendum continues while the Member's membership and occupancy agreement are in force. It ends
automatically if membership ends. Sections on settlement, records and liability survive.
