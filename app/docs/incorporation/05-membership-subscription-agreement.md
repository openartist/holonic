## 1. Parties

This agreement is between **Holons Coop**, a California nonprofit mutual benefit corporation
operating as a cooperative (the "Co-op"), and the member named above (the "Member").

## 2. What the Member is buying

2.1 **Membership.** The Member is applying to become a member of the Co-op with the right to use one
recreational vehicle space in the Co-op's park during the approved season, subject to the occupancy
and use agreement and the Co-op's rules.

2.2 **Capital contribution.** The Member will contribute the amount stated above to the Co-op's
capital for the development of the park. The contribution is applied to approved project costs and
is not rent, not a deposit and not a loan.

2.3 **Site-license tier.** The tier reflects the location, shade, privacy and convenience of the
space the Member may be assigned. It does not create a different vote, a greater ownership share,
a deed, a lease or a guaranteed specific space.

2.4 **No real property interest.** The Member receives no deed, no recorded lot, no tenancy, no
right of residency and no interest in the Co-op's land. The Member's rights are contractual and
subject to the Co-op's approved use, permits and rules.

2.5 **Dues.** The Member will pay monthly dues of $150, adjustable by the board on at least 30 days'
notice, to fund the baseline operating budget.

## 3. Admission

3.1 The board approves admissions. The Member becomes a member when this agreement and the
occupancy, disclosure and rental-pool documents are signed, the contribution and any deposit are
cleared, and the board records the admission in the membership ledger.

3.2 The Co-op may decline an application for a lawful reason stated in writing. If it declines, any
contribution received is returned without interest, less any costs the Co-op has actually incurred
for that application as disclosed in Schedule B.

3.3 The Member's contribution is held in the Co-op's account and is not segregated unless counsel
requires it. The Co-op may use it for approved project costs before completion, and the Member
accepts that risk.

## 4. Capital at risk

4.1 The Member understands that the contribution is at risk. The Co-op may not complete the park,
may change its plans, may face cost overruns, or may be unable to obtain a permit to operate.

4.2 The contribution is not insured, not guaranteed, and not redeemable on demand.

4.3 The board may adopt a redemption policy. Any redemption is subject to the Co-op's reserves,
debts, approved budget and California law, and may be paid over time or declined.

## 5. Use rights and rules

5.1 The Member's use is governed by the occupancy and use agreement, the bylaws, the rules adopted
by the board and the conditions of the Co-op's permits.

5.2 The Member must comply with occupancy limits, vehicle and parking rules, quiet hours, fire
restrictions, water and waste rules, and event conditions.

5.3 The Member may not sublet, assign or license the space, and may not advertise it for rent,
except through the Co-op's rental pool under the addendum.

5.4 The Co-op may suspend use for maintenance, safety, agency action or nonpayment, as the
occupancy agreement provides.

## 6. Rental pool

6.1 Participation is voluntary. If the Member signs the rental-pool addendum, the Member may release
unused space-nights for guest stays and receive 60% of lodging receipts for those nights, before
agreed operating costs, with the Co-op retaining 40%.

6.2 The pool is not a promise of bookings, income or a particular payout. The Co-op may suspend
the pool for maintenance, insurance or agency requirements.

6.3 The Co-op collects and remits transient occupancy tax on taxable stays. The Member is
responsible for the Member's own income tax treatment of payouts.

## 7. Voting and governance

7.1 Each member has one vote, regardless of tier or contribution.

7.2 Members elect directors and vote on the matters the bylaws and California law reserve to them,
including material changes to dues, contributions, transfer and refund terms, dissolution and the
cooperative provisions.

## 8. Transfer, resignation and termination

8.1 Membership is not transferable without board approval. A transferee must qualify for admission
and pay any transfer fee the board sets.

8.2 A member may resign in writing. Resignation does not create a refund right.

8.3 The board may terminate membership for nonpayment beyond 30 days after notice, breach of the
rules, endangerment of others, or material misrepresentation. The Member may respond in writing
before a final decision.

## 9. Member representations

The Member represents that:

- The Member has received and read the disclosure statement and the current bylaws.
- The Member has had the opportunity to ask questions and consult independent counsel and tax
  advisers.
- The Member can bear the loss of the contribution.
- The Member is not relying on any statement about approvals, construction timing, occupancy,
  rental income or resale value.
- The Member is at least 18 years old and is acquiring the membership for personal use, not for
  resale or investment.

## 10. Disclosures and acknowledgments

The Member acknowledges that:

1. No county, health, fire or park approval has been obtained for the Member's occupancy.
2. No public report or permit for the offering has been issued, and counsel's DRE and securities
   analysis is not complete.
3. The Co-op's budget is illustrative and may change materially.
4. Dues may increase and special assessments may be proposed.
5. The Member has no deed, no lease and no right to reside permanently.
6. Membership may be illiquid and difficult to resell.

## 11. Dispute resolution

The parties will first attempt mediation in Imperial County, California. If mediation does not
resolve the dispute within 60 days, the dispute will be resolved by binding arbitration before a
single arbitrator under the rules of the American Arbitration Association, seated in Imperial
County. Either party may seek injunctive relief in court to protect the property or safety. The
Member waives a jury trial to the extent the law permits.

## 12. General

12.1 **Notices.** Notices go to the addresses in the membership ledger and are effective on
delivery or three days after mailing.

12.2 **Entire agreement.** This agreement, the occupancy agreement, the disclosure statement, the
bylaws and the rules are the entire agreement and supersede prior discussions.

12.3 **Severability.** If a provision is unenforceable, the rest continues in effect.

12.4 **Governing law.** California law governs.

12.5 **Counterparts.** This agreement may be signed electronically and in counterparts.
