**B-2. Principal office.** The principal office is fixed at [street address], [city], California
[ZIP], in the County of Imperial. The board may change it by resolution.

**B-3. Fiscal year.** The fiscal year begins on 1 January and ends on 31 December.

**B-4. Corporate records.** The secretary shall keep the articles, bylaws, minutes, consents, the
membership ledger, the director and officer register and the other records required by law in the
corporate record book, at the principal office or another place the board designates.

**B-5. Bank account.** The treasurer is authorized to open and maintain accounts at [bank name],
and to execute the bank's account documents. Checks and electronic payments above $[____] require
two approvals as stated in the financial controls policy. The board may change banks by resolution.

**B-6. EIN.** The president and treasurer are authorized to apply for an employer identification
number on IRS Form SS-4, using the corporation's legal name and the responsible-party information
provided to the board.

**B-7. Statement of Information.** The secretary is authorized to file Form SI-100 with the
California Secretary of State within 90 days of incorporation and on the state's subsequent
biennial schedule, and to keep the confirmation in the record book.

**B-8. Membership program.** The board adopts the membership program in the study and bylaws:

| Tier | Capital contribution | Memberships | Total |
|------|----------------------|-------------|-------|
| Standard | $7,500 | 8 | $60,000 |
| Preferred | $10,000 | 8 | $80,000 |
| Premium | $12,500 | 4 | $50,000 |
| **Total** | | **20** | **$190,000** |

Monthly dues are $150 per member. The board offers resident (year-round), seasonal and absent-owner
occupancy types on the terms in the occupancy agreement; residential occupancy is offered only
where the county and the approved use permit it. Each contribution purchases one membership share in the
corporation, recorded in the membership ledger; the corporation holds title to the parcel and
improvements for the benefit of its members. Membership admissions require board approval, payment
in full, and signed subscription, occupancy, disclosure and rental-pool documents as applicable. No
membership may be offered until counsel has completed the DRE and securities analysis.

**B-9. Policies adopted.** The following policies are approved and adopted, and the secretary shall
file them with these resolutions:

- Conflict of Interest Policy (`04`).
- Records and Meetings Policy (`10`).
- Financial Controls and Dues Policy (`11`).
- Membership Subscription Agreement (`05`), for use after counsel review.
- Occupancy and Use Agreement (`06`), for use after counsel review.
- Rental Pool Addendum (`07`) and Guest Booking Terms (`08`), for use after counsel review.
- Disclosure Statement (`09`), for use after counsel review.

**B-10. Rental pool terms.** The board authorizes a managed rental pool on the terms in the bylaws:
60% of lodging receipts to the releasing member, 40% to the corporation, before agreed operating
costs, with transient occupancy tax collected and remitted as a liability. The general manager may
adjust the operating calendar within the approved season.

**B-11. Insurance.** The treasurer is authorized to bind park, general liability, property,
directors and officers, and event insurance, subject to the budget and to county confirmation of
the approved use. Coverage must be in place before any member or guest occupies a space.

**B-12. Professional advisors.** The board authorizes engagement of counsel for entity, offering
and DRE analysis, and a CPA for tax classification, payroll and owner-payout reporting, within the
approved budget.

**B-13. Transient occupancy tax.** The treasurer is authorized to register with the Imperial County
Tax Collector, collect the published 8% transient occupancy tax on taxable stays, hold it as a
liability and remit it on the required schedule.

**B-14. County and health filings.** The president is authorized to submit the county use inquiry,
water and wastewater applications, fire access review and food-service classification requests, and
to report the responses to the board before construction commitments are made.

**B-15. Opening conditions.** No space may be occupied and no booking may be accepted until: the
county has confirmed the use in writing; water and wastewater have written approval; a permit to
operate has been issued; insurance is bound; and the board has adopted an opening budget with a
funded reserve.

**B-16. Reimbursement policy.** Directors and officers may be reimbursed for reasonable, documented
expenses incurred for the corporation, approved in advance by the treasurer and reported to the
board. No compensation is paid for board service.

**B-17. Compensation and conflicts.** No director or officer may vote on a contract in which the
director or officer has a material interest. Interested-party transactions require disclosure,
recusal and a board finding that the terms are fair and in the corporation's interest.

**B-18. Ratification of expenditures.** All formation expenditures to date, including filing fees,
counsel fees, diligence costs and insurance deposits, are ratified and shall be recorded in the
corporate books.

**B-19. Secured borrowing and receivables.** The president and treasurer are authorised to negotiate
and execute, on a board resolution under Bylaws Article 5.5, a secured facility secured by the
corporation's real property or its payment intangibles — including dues and rental-pool receipts —
together with any assignment of receivables, lockbox or deposit account control agreement the lender
requires. No such instrument may be signed before the board records the collateral, the coverage
ratio and the member disclosure the financial controls policy requires.
