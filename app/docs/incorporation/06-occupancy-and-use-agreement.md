# Occupancy and Use Agreement — Holons Coop

**Occupancy type:** [Resident / Seasonal / Absent-owner]  **Season or term:** [____]

> DRAFT — counsel review required before use. This agreement sets how a member occupies a space.
> Ownership is the membership share in the Co-op. Seasonal and absent-owner occupancy are licences;
> resident occupancy is a tenancy under California law and needs its own approved terms.

## 1. The membership share and the licence to occupy

1.1 The Member owns a membership share in the Co-op, which owns the parcel and the improvements on
it. This agreement sets the Member's right to occupy one recreational vehicle space, together with
the shared facilities, on the terms of this agreement and the occupancy type selected above.

1.2 **Seasonal and absent-owner occupancy** are licences to use the space. For those occupancy
types this agreement does not create a landlord-tenant relationship, a leasehold in a specific lot,
a mobilehome or manufactured-home tenancy, or any interest in land.

1.3 **Resident occupancy** is the Member's year-round home and is a tenancy under California law. It
is offered only for spaces and periods the Co-op's approved use permits as residential, and its
notice, termination and habitability terms are those the law requires. A resident Member may
receive mail at the space and use the address as a residence.

1.4 The Member holds one occupancy type at a time. A change of occupancy type requires the Co-op's
written approval and any approval the change requires from the county or another agency.

## 2. Term and season

2.1 **Seasonal occupancy** runs for the season stated above, which the Co-op operates for up to 180
nights per year. The board sets the opening and closing dates and may shorten the season for
weather, maintenance, water supply, fire, agency action or insurance requirements.

2.2 **Resident occupancy** runs year-round for the term stated above and renews as the rules
provide. It is available only where the approved use permits residential occupancy.

2.3 Seasonal stays are limited to the maximum consecutive and annual nights stated in the rules and
required by the Co-op's permits. A seasonal Member may not occupy outside the approved season.

2.4 **Absent-owner occupancy** is a resident or seasonal space whose Member is away. The Member may
release nights through the rental pool under the addendum and may not otherwise let the space.

2.5 The Co-op may close the park temporarily without refunding dues where closure is required for
safety, utilities, fire or agency action, but will credit prepaid rental-pool amounts for affected
bookings.

2.6 **Resident tenancy terms.** For resident occupancy the Co-op states rent and dues separately,
gives the notices California law requires for a rent increase, and maintains the space and shared
systems in the condition the law requires. A security deposit may not exceed one month's rent, is
held as the law requires, and is returned with an itemised statement.

2.7 **Ending a resident tenancy.** The Co-op may end a resident tenancy without cause only on 60
days' written notice, and for cause only on the grounds and notice the law allows, through the
procedure the law requires. The Member may end it on the notice the law requires.

2.8 **Entry to a resident space.** The Co-op gives at least 24 hours' written notice before entry
except in an emergency, and enters only to repair, inspect, maintain or for another lawful purpose.

## 3. Space and occupancy

3.1 The Member is assigned a space by the Co-op. The Co-op may reassign the space for maintenance,
design changes, safety or permit conditions, and will give notice where practicable.

3.2 The Member may occupy the space with the Member's self-contained recreational vehicle and the
occupants listed on the member record. The occupancy limit is [__] persons per space, including
children, unless the rules allow otherwise.

3.3 Only self-contained RVs approved by the Co-op may be used. Tents, temporary structures, sheds,
fences and exterior storage are not permitted without written approval.

3.4 The Member must keep the space clean, remove trash, and avoid accumulating equipment, fuel or
materials that create a fire, health or safety risk.

## 4. Vehicles and parking

4.1 The Member may keep one recreational vehicle and one towed or towing vehicle on the assigned
space where the space allows. Additional vehicles must use shared parking.

4.2 Shared parking is at the edge of the park and near the entrance. Parking is never internal to
the residential areas. The Co-op provides 24 resident spaces and 4 visitor spaces; the Member may
not park on circulation routes, fire lanes, accessible spaces without a permit, or unassigned
ground.

4.3 Vehicles must be operable, registered and insured. The Co-op may remove or tow a vehicle that
blocks access, creates a hazard or is abandoned, at the owner's expense as the law allows.

## 5. Utilities and services

5.1 Water, wastewater, power and waste service are provided as the Co-op's approved systems allow.
The Member must not connect to, alter or extend any utility without written approval.

5.2 The Member must conserve water, report leaks, and follow the waste and greywater rules. Dumping
waste outside designated points is prohibited.

5.3 Power is provided for the Member's RV and ordinary use. The Member may not use the space for
commercial charging, mining, manufacturing or any load the system was not designed for.

## 6. Shared facilities

6.1 The hall, kitchen, showers, toilets, stage and outdoor areas are shared. The Member must follow
posted hours, cleaning rules, food-safety rules and event conditions.

6.2 The Member may reserve shared facilities only through the Co-op's procedure. Events require
written approval, insurance where required, and compliance with occupancy, noise and fire limits.

6.3 The Member is responsible for damage caused by the Member, the Member's guests or the Member's
vehicles, beyond ordinary wear.

## 7. Guests

7.1 The Member may host guests within the occupancy limit and the rules. Guests must register, obey
the rules and park in visitor spaces.

7.2 Guests may not stay beyond the guest limit without the Co-op's written approval and payment of
any applicable guest fee or transient occupancy tax.

7.3 The Member is responsible for the conduct of the Member's guests.

## 8. Rental of the space

8.1 The Member may not rent, sublet or advertise the space except through the Co-op's rental pool
under a signed addendum. A Member who is away, including a resident Member travelling, may release
nights through the pool.

8.2 Any approved guest stay through the pool is subject to the guest booking terms and the
collection and remission of transient occupancy tax.

## 9. Conduct and quiet enjoyment

9.1 The Member must respect neighbours, keep noise reasonable, observe quiet hours, and follow fire
and heat restrictions.

9.2 Generators are permitted only during posted hours and where the rules allow. Open fires,
fireworks, weapons and illegal substances are prohibited.

9.3 The Co-op may suspend or terminate a licence, and may terminate a resident tenancy on the
grounds and notice the law allows, for a serious breach, repeated breaches, endangerment, or
nonpayment, following the notice procedure in the bylaws.

## 10. Maintenance and access

10.1 The Co-op may enter the space to inspect, repair, maintain utilities, respond to an emergency
or comply with an agency requirement, with reasonable notice except in an emergency.

10.2 The Member must report damage promptly and must not repair Co-op property without approval.

10.3 The Member is responsible for the Member's own RV, equipment and personal property. The Co-op
does not provide security for them and is not an insurer.

## 11. Insurance and liability

11.1 The Member must maintain insurance on the Member's RV and vehicles as required by law and the
rules, and provide evidence on request.

11.2 The Member is responsible for loss or injury caused by the Member, the Member's guests or the
Member's property.

11.3 To the extent permitted by law, the Member releases the Co-op from liability for loss or injury
that is not caused by the Co-op's negligence or wilful misconduct, and agrees to indemnify the
Co-op for claims arising from the Member's use.

## 12. Occupancy types and residency

12.1 A seasonal Member has no right of residency. Resident occupancy is offered only for spaces and
periods the county and the Co-op's approved use permit as residential, and only on the terms this
agreement and California law require.

12.2 The Co-op makes no representation that residential occupancy will be approved for any space,
and a change of occupancy type requires written approval.

12.3 Manufactured homes and deeded lots remain separate products that require their own approvals,
financing and agreements, and are not offered by this agreement.

## 13. Compliance and termination

13.1 The Member must comply with county, health, fire and park requirements and with the Co-op's
rules, as amended on notice.

13.2 The Co-op may terminate a seasonal or absent-owner licence on written notice for material
breach, nonpayment, endangerment or loss of a required permit. A resident tenancy may be terminated
only on the grounds and notice California law allows and only through the procedure the law
requires. The Member must remove the RV and property within the stated period.

13.3 Termination of the licence does not automatically terminate membership, and termination of
membership ends the licence.

13.4 **Assignment of occupancy charges.** The Member's obligation to pay dues, occupancy charges and
other amounts under this agreement may be assigned to a lender as collateral, and the Co-op may
direct payment into a lockbox or controlled account. The Co-op will give the Member notice of any
assignment that changes where payment is made. An assignment does not change the Member's rights or
the Co-op's obligations under this agreement.

## 14. Disputes and governing law

The dispute procedure in the membership subscription agreement applies. California law governs.
