# Records and Meetings Policy — Holons Coop

Adopted by the board of directors on [date].

> Draft for board adoption. This policy sets the minimum record-keeping standard; counsel and the
> CPA may require more.

## 1. Purpose

The corporation must be able to show what it decided, who decided it, what it owns and owes, and
what it told members. This policy sets out what is kept, where, for how long, who may see it, and
how meetings are noticed and recorded.

## 3. Minutes

Minutes are not a transcript. Each set must record:

- The entity name, meeting type, date, time and place.
- Who attended and whether a quorum was present, and who presided and took minutes.
- Each motion, who moved and seconded it, and the vote count.
- A summary of material discussion, including alternatives considered.
- Conflicts disclosed and recusals, with the interested person named.
- Attachments and reports received, by title and date.
- The next meeting date if set, and the adjournment time.
- The secretary's signature once approved.

Minutes are circulated to directors within [__] days and approved at the next meeting. Written
consents are filed with the minutes they replace.

## 4. Notices and consents

4.1 Member meeting notice is given at least 10 and not more than 90 days before the meeting, stating
the date, time, place and matters to be voted on. Notice may be delivered personally, by mail or by
electronic transmission consented to by the member.

4.2 Board meeting notice is given at least 48 hours before a special meeting, and on the adopted
schedule for regular meetings. Notice may be waived in writing or by attendance without objection.

4.3 Written consent of all directors may act in place of a meeting. A consent is filed with the
minutes and must state the action taken and the date.

4.4 Member action by written ballot must state the proposed action, the deadline and the number of
votes needed, and must give members a reasonable opportunity to vote.

## 5. Member inspection

5.1 A member may inspect the articles, bylaws, minutes of member meetings, the membership ledger
with privacy redactions, and the annual financial report, for a proper purpose and on reasonable
written notice.

5.2 The board may require the member to state the purpose and to sign a confidentiality undertaking
for member contact information. The corporation may charge the reasonable cost of copies.

5.3 The corporation may withhold records protected by law, privilege, privacy or a signed
confidentiality agreement, and may redact information about other members, guests, staff or
applicants.

## 6. Privacy and guest data

Member and guest data is used for membership administration, bookings, safety, tax and insurance. It
is not sold. Access is limited to people who need it. Contact details are shared with a member only
as needed to prepare for that member's booked stay. A request to correct personal information is
answered in writing.

## 7. Electronic records and signatures

Records may be kept electronically if they are readable, complete, backed up and retrievable.
Electronic signatures are accepted where the law permits, with the signer's identity and intent
recorded. Board and member meetings may be held by video or telephone if everyone can hear one
another and the minutes record the method.

## 8. Annual report to members

Within 120 days after the fiscal year ends, the board gives members a financial report showing
revenue by source, operating expenses, reserves, owner-pool settlements, TOT collected and remitted,
and any material legal or insurance developments. The report need not be audited unless the members
or a lender require it.

## 9. Retention schedule

| Record | Retention |
|--------|-----------|
| Articles, bylaws, minute books, member ledger | Permanent |
| Financial statements and budgets | 7 years |
| Tax returns, TOT returns and remittances | 7 years after filing |
| Contracts and insurance policies | 7 years after expiry |
| Conflict statements and recusals | 7 years |
| Guest bookings and rental statements | 7 years |
| Incident and safety reports | 7 years, or longer for a claim |
| Routine correspondence | 3 years |
| Superseded drafts | Until the final version is adopted, then 1 year |

Destruction is suspended while a claim, audit, investigation or litigation hold is active.

## 10. Security and backup

The record book is kept in a locked cabinet or an access-controlled folder. Financial and member
data is backed up at least weekly, with one copy off-site or in a reputable cloud service. Access
credentials are held by the secretary and treasurer and changed when an officer leaves. A breach or
loss is reported to the board at its next meeting.

## 11. Review

The board reviews this policy annually, and after any change of accountant, bank, insurance or
management. The secretary certifies the review in the minutes.
