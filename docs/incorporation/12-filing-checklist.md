# Filing Checklist — Holons Coop

Use this list to assemble the packet. Each row needs an owner, a date and the evidence filed in the
record book. Status values: **open**, **in progress**, **done**.

> Draft working checklist. Fees and forms must be confirmed with the agency at the time of filing.

## A. Before filing the articles

| # | Item | Owner | Status | Evidence |
|---|------|-------|--------|----------|
| A1 | Search the Secretary of State name database for "Holons Coop" | Secretary | open | Search result PDF |
| A2 | Counsel confirms use of "cooperative" / "co-op" in the name | Counsel | open | Written memo |
| A3 | Reserve the name (optional) | Secretary | open | Reservation confirmation |
| A4 | Registered agent consent, physical California address | Secretary | open | Signed consent |
| A5 | Principal office street address in Imperial County | Board | open | Address resolution |

| A7 | Three initial directors willing to serve | Board | open | Director register |
| A8 | Counsel review of articles and bylaws | Counsel | open | Marked-up drafts |
