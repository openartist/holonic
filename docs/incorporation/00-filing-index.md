# Filing Index — Holons Coop

**Entity:** Holons Coop, a California nonprofit mutual benefit corporation operating as a
consumer cooperative (Corp. Code § 12200 et seq.), proposed.
**Jurisdiction:** California. **Principal office:** County of Imperial.
**Prepared:** 8 November 2026. **Packet version:** 1.0.

> This is a working draft prepared from the Holons Coop purchase and formation study. It is
> not legal advice, has not been reviewed by counsel, and has not been filed. Bracketed blanks
> are completed at signing. No membership interest is offered by this packet.

## What this packet is

A filing-ready set of incorporation and membership documents for one member-owned park on one
co-op-owned parcel, following the structure recommended in the study: a co-op corporation with
a board that delegates daily operations to a general manager. It covers the Secretary of State
filing, the EIN, the organizational meeting, the membership and occupancy agreements, the
rental pool, and the policies the board must adopt before a member is admitted.

## Open items before filing

1. **Name clearance.** Search the Secretary of State name database and have counsel confirm that
   "Holons Coop" may be used, given the statutory restrictions on "cooperative" and "co-op".
2. **Registered agent.** A California resident or registered corporate agent with a physical
   street address in the state, plus a signed consent.
3. **Initial directors.** Three individuals willing to serve until the first member election,
   with names and addresses.
4. **Incorporators.** Three signatories, who may be the same people as the initial directors.
5. **Principal office address.** A street address in Imperial County for the record; a mailing
   address may differ.
6. **EIN responsible party.** One individual's name and taxpayer identification number.
7. **Counsel sign-off.** Articles, bylaws and the offering documents before any solicitation.
8. **DRE and securities analysis.** Before memberships are marketed, as the study requires.
9. **County use confirmation and water/wastewater approval.** Before closing and before any
   occupancy commitment.
10. **Insurance.** Park, general liability, D&O and event cover quoted before opening.

## Assembly and filing notes

- Print on white letter stock, single-sided, with each exhibit labelled. File the articles
  online or by mail with the Secretary of State; keep the filed stamped copy and the filing
  receipt in the corporate record book.
- Adopt the bylaws and organizational resolutions at one meeting, with all initial directors
  present, and keep signed minutes and a written consent in the record book.
- File the SI-100 within 90 days of incorporation, then on the state's biennial schedule.
- Never collect a membership contribution before the offering documents have cleared counsel
  review. Hold contributions in a segregated account until admission is complete.
- Treat collected transient occupancy tax as a liability, not revenue, and remit on the county
  schedule.
