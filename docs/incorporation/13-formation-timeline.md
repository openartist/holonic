## Statutory deadlines

| Filing | Deadline | Consequence if missed |
|--------|----------|-----------------------|
| Articles of incorporation | Before any corporate act | No entity |
| SI-100 | Within 90 days of incorporation | Penalty; possible suspension |
| EIN | Before opening accounts or hiring | Cannot bank or report |
| Annual member meeting | Within 120 days after fiscal year end | Governance gap |
| Federal and state returns | 15th day of the 5th month after year end | Penalties and interest |
| TOT returns | County schedule | Penalties and interest |
| Biennial SI-100 | State schedule | Penalty; possible suspension |

## Critical path

1. Name clearance → articles → EIN → bank account.
2. Articles → organisational meeting → bylaws, officers, policies.
3. Bylaws → counsel offering analysis → membership documents → first admissions.
4. County use inquiry → water and wastewater → fire → permits → insurance → opening.

No membership payment and no guest stay may happen before the corresponding gate is cleared.
