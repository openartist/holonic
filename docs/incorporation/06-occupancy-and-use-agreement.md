# Occupancy and Use Agreement — Holons Coop

**Member:** [________________]  **Space assigned:** [____]  **Season:** [____]

> DRAFT — counsel review required before use. This agreement grants a licence to use a space; it
> does not create a lease, tenancy or residency right.

## 1. Licence, not lease

1.1 The Co-op grants the Member a revocable licence to occupy one recreational vehicle space for
seasonal recreational use, together with the shared facilities, on the terms of this agreement.

1.2 This agreement does not create a landlord-tenant relationship, a leasehold, a right of
residency, a mobilehome or manufactured-home tenancy, or any interest in real property.

1.3 The Member may not claim the space as a residence, receive mail there as a residence, register
to vote there, or use the address for any purpose that implies permanent occupancy.

## 2. Term and season

2.1 The licence runs for the season stated above, which the Co-op operates for up to 180 nights per
year. The board sets the opening and closing dates and may shorten the season for weather,
maintenance, water supply, fire, agency action or insurance requirements.

2.2 Stays are limited to the maximum consecutive and annual nights stated in the rules and required
by the Co-op's permits. The Member may not occupy outside the approved season.

2.3 The Co-op may close the park temporarily without refunding dues where closure is required for
safety, utilities, fire or agency action, but will credit prepaid rental-pool amounts for affected
bookings.

## 3. Space and occupancy

3.1 The Member is assigned a space by the Co-op. The Co-op may reassign the space for maintenance,
design changes, safety or permit conditions, and will give notice where practicable.

3.2 The Member may occupy the space with the Member's self-contained recreational vehicle and the
occupants listed on the member record. The occupancy limit is [__] persons per space, including
children, unless the rules allow otherwise.

3.3 Only self-contained RVs approved by the Co-op may be used. Tents, temporary structures, sheds,
fences and exterior storage are not permitted without written approval.

3.4 The Member must keep the space clean, remove trash, and avoid accumulating equipment, fuel or
materials that create a fire, health or safety risk.

## 4. Vehicles and parking

4.1 The Member may keep one recreational vehicle and one towed or towing vehicle on the assigned
space where the space allows. Additional vehicles must use shared parking.

4.2 Shared parking is at the edge of the park and near the entrance. Parking is never internal to
the residential areas. The Co-op provides 24 resident spaces and 4 visitor spaces; the Member may
not park on circulation routes, fire lanes, accessible spaces without a permit, or unassigned
ground.

4.3 Vehicles must be operable, registered and insured. The Co-op may remove or tow a vehicle that
blocks access, creates a hazard or is abandoned, at the owner's expense as the law allows.

## 5. Utilities and services

5.1 Water, wastewater, power and waste service are provided as the Co-op's approved systems allow.
The Member must not connect to, alter or extend any utility without written approval.

5.2 The Member must conserve water, report leaks, and follow the waste and greywater rules. Dumping
waste outside designated points is prohibited.

5.3 Power is provided for the Member's RV and ordinary use. The Member may not use the space for
commercial charging, mining, manufacturing or any load the system was not designed for.

## 6. Shared facilities

6.1 The hall, kitchen, showers, toilets, stage and outdoor areas are shared. The Member must follow
posted hours, cleaning rules, food-safety rules and event conditions.

6.2 The Member may reserve shared facilities only through the Co-op's procedure. Events require
written approval, insurance where required, and compliance with occupancy, noise and fire limits.

6.3 The Member is responsible for damage caused by the Member, the Member's guests or the Member's
vehicles, beyond ordinary wear.

## 7. Guests

7.1 The Member may host guests within the occupancy limit and the rules. Guests must register, obey
the rules and park in visitor spaces.

7.2 Guests may not stay beyond the guest limit without the Co-op's written approval and payment of
any applicable guest fee or transient occupancy tax.

7.3 The Member is responsible for the conduct of the Member's guests.

## 8. Rental of the space

8.1 The Member may not rent, sublet or advertise the space except through the Co-op's rental pool
under a signed addendum.

8.2 Any approved guest stay through the pool is subject to the guest booking terms and the
collection and remission of transient occupancy tax.

## 9. Conduct and quiet enjoyment

9.1 The Member must respect neighbours, keep noise reasonable, observe quiet hours, and follow fire
and heat restrictions.

9.2 Generators are permitted only during posted hours and where the rules allow. Open fires,
fireworks, weapons and illegal substances are prohibited.

9.3 The Co-op may suspend or terminate the licence for a serious breach, repeated breaches,
endangerment, or nonpayment, following the notice procedure in the bylaws.

## 10. Maintenance and access

10.1 The Co-op may enter the space to inspect, repair, maintain utilities, respond to an emergency
or comply with an agency requirement, with reasonable notice except in an emergency.

10.2 The Member must report damage promptly and must not repair Co-op property without approval.

10.3 The Member is responsible for the Member's own RV, equipment and personal property. The Co-op
does not provide security for them and is not an insurer.

## 11. Insurance and liability

11.1 The Member must maintain insurance on the Member's RV and vehicles as required by law and the
rules, and provide evidence on request.

11.2 The Member is responsible for loss or injury caused by the Member, the Member's guests or the
Member's property.

11.3 To the extent permitted by law, the Member releases the Co-op from liability for loss or injury
that is not caused by the Co-op's negligence or wilful misconduct, and agrees to indemnify the
Co-op for claims arising from the Member's use.

## 12. No residency and no permanent occupancy

The Member acknowledges that the park is approved for seasonal recreational use only, that the Co-op
makes no representation that residency is permitted, and that any change to permanent residency
would require separate approvals and a separate agreement.

## 13. Compliance and termination

13.1 The Member must comply with county, health, fire and park requirements and with the Co-op's
rules, as amended on notice.

13.2 The Co-op may terminate this licence on written notice for material breach, nonpayment,
endangerment or loss of a required permit. The Member must remove the RV and property within the
stated period.

13.3 Termination of the licence does not automatically terminate membership, and termination of
membership ends the licence.

## 14. Disputes and governing law

The dispute procedure in the membership subscription agreement applies. California law governs.
