A California nonprofit mutual benefit corporation with cooperative provisions.
Prepared for filing on Form ARTS-MB with the California Secretary of State.

The undersigned incorporators, acting under the California Nonprofit Corporation Law, adopt the
following articles of incorporation.

## Article III — Purposes

The specific and primary purposes of this corporation are:

1. To own, hold, develop and operate a member-owned seasonal recreational vehicle community and
   shared community facilities on property owned by the corporation, for the mutual benefit of its
   members and their guests.
2. To provide shared water, wastewater, power, parking, pedestrian circulation and community
   buildings, and to maintain them for the common use of members.
3. To operate a managed rental pool through which members may release unused space-nights for
   guest stays, and to manage small retreats and events where the responsible agencies approve
   those activities.
4. To carry on any other lawful activity for which a nonprofit mutual benefit corporation may be
   organized, consistent with the cooperative character of the corporation.

The corporation shall not be organized for the private gain of any person. No part of its net
earnings shall inure to the benefit of any director, officer or member except as permitted by the
California Nonprofit Corporation Law and these articles, including patronage allocations
authorized under Article VIII.

## Article IV — Members

4.1 **One class of membership.** The corporation shall have one class of members. Every member in
good standing has equal voting rights: one member, one vote, regardless of the site-license tier
or capital contribution the member selected.

4.2 **Admission.** Membership is open to natural persons who apply in writing, are approved by the
board, pay the capital contribution and dues then in effect, and sign the membership subscription
agreement, the occupancy and use agreement and the disclosure acknowledgment.

4.3 **Site-license tiers.** The board may offer Standard, Preferred and Premium site-license tiers
at different capital contributions to reflect location, shade, privacy and convenience. A tier is a
license characteristic only. It confers no additional vote, no greater ownership percentage and no
deeded interest in any space.

4.4 **Dues and contributions.** The board sets dues, capital contributions and any capital call,
subject to the approval requirements in the bylaws. Membership contributions are capital funding,
not rent and not a guaranteed refundable deposit.

4.5 **No deeds, no tenancy.** Membership does not convey a recorded lot, a leasehold, a tenancy, a
right of residency or any interest in real property. Members receive a contractual right to use a
space under the occupancy and use agreement, subject to the rules and to the corporation's
approved use.

4.6 **Transfer and termination.** Membership is not transferable except as the bylaws provide.
Membership terminates on resignation, nonpayment after notice, or removal for cause under the
bylaws.

## Article VIII — Cooperative Provisions

8.1 **Cooperative character.** The corporation elects to operate on a cooperative basis. Control
is vested in the members on a one-member, one-vote basis. Capital is contributed by members.
Services are provided at cost, with any surplus allocated or retained as the board determines for
the benefit of the membership and the reserve.

8.2 **Patronage allocations.** The board may allocate net surplus to members on the basis of
patronage, including dues paid and space-nights released, and may pay, credit or retain those
allocations, subject to California law, the bylaws, the corporation's reserves and any applicable
tax treatment. No allocation is a promise of a distribution.

8.3 **Dissolution.** On dissolution after payment of debts and liabilities, remaining assets shall
be distributed to the members in accordance with the bylaws and California law, or transferred to
one or more nonprofit or cooperative organizations if the members so approve. No distribution may
be made in contravention of the California Nonprofit Corporation Law.

8.4 **Amendment.** Articles VIII and Article IV may be amended only with the approval of the
members as the bylaws require.

## Article IX — Limitation of Liability and Indemnification

To the fullest extent permitted by the California Nonprofit Corporation Law, no director or
officer shall be personally liable for monetary damages for acts or omissions in that capacity, and
the corporation shall indemnify its directors, officers and agents against expenses and liabilities
incurred in connection with the corporation's affairs. The corporation may purchase directors and
officers liability insurance. These provisions do not eliminate liability for intentional
misconduct, self-dealing or any other matter that cannot be limited by law.

## Article X — Tax Status

The corporation may apply for recognition of exemption from federal and state income tax if the
board determines that it qualifies. No exemption is assumed by these articles, and no statement in
this document is tax advice. Membership contributions, dues, rental receipts and owner payouts
require CPA review for classification and reporting.

## Article XI — Bylaws

The board shall adopt bylaws consistent with these articles for the governance of the corporation.
In the event of a conflict, these articles control.
