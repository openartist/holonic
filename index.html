Holons · about

A network of cohousing co-ops, built one place at a time.

Holons is a network of resident-owned places. Each project holds its own land, membership contract, dues and land rights, while sharing one membership path across the network.

01

One owner record

Your membership travels with you across Holons, subject to each project’s contract, local rules and availability.

02

Local control

Every Holon sets its own membership types, dues, land rights, governance and stay limits. Nothing is copied from another project.

03

Built in the open

The site plan, calculator, financial study and legal drafts stay visible while each project is in formation.

Holons · mission

Make belonging a form of ownership.

We are building small, resident-owned places where the land, the shared spaces and the decisions stay with the people who live there.

01

Resident ownership

Members hold a real stake in the place they live, with clear dues, land rights and a written path into the co-op.

02

Shared stewardship

Each village holds common land and shared infrastructure together, while every household keeps a private place of its own.

03

A clear path in

From the first project tour to the signed application and agreement, the steps, costs and open questions stay visible.

What we are testing

Can a small co-op make ownership simpler?

Lithium City is the working study: one parcel, a live plan, a transparent financial model and a reservation process that shows every document before signature.
